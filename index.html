Chopper Run

Outrun the helicopters.

How to play

Helicopters hunt you. Survive as long as you can. Every 25 seconds it gets harder.

Stay alive

  • Buildings stop bullets
  • Trees, bushes and tall grass hide you, but not for long
  • Get out of red circles: rockets
  • Red laser: a sniper. Roll or take cover
  • Cars are fast, but they always see you
