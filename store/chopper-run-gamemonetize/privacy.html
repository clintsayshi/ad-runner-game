Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

What we collect

Only if you click "OK" on the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see how the game is played: when a game starts and ends, how long you lasted, the level you reached and whether you are on a phone. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser and device type. We do not get your name or email.

If you click "No thanks", Google Analytics stores no cookies.

Error reports

If the game crashes, a report with the error, your browser and device type may be sent to Sentry so we can fix it. It holds no personal details.

Stored on your device

The game saves your best time, which tips you have seen, and your cookie choice in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device.

Ads

If the game shows video ads, the ad provider may use cookies and device data under its own privacy policy.

Your choice

To change your cookie choice, clear this site's data in your browser and reload. To ask about your data, email you@example.com.

Third parties: Google, Sentry.
