How to play Chopper Run 3D

Attack helicopters are hunting you. Stay alive as long as you can. Every 25 seconds it gets harder: more choppers, better aim, rockets, then snipers.

Play now – free

On foot

W A S D / arrowsRun (mouse to look, click to lock)
ShiftSprint (uses stamina)
CDuck: smaller target, hides you in bushes and tall grass
SpaceDive roll: nothing can hit you mid-roll
JJump
EJump into a manhole and come out across town
QSmoke bomb
FGet in or out of a car
M / PSound on/off, pause

Driving

W / SGas / brake
A / DSteer
SpaceHandbrake
Shift or XGear up
Ctrl or ZGear down (reverse only when stopped)

Tips

Buildings stop bullets, so keep one between you and a chopper. Hiding only lasts so long: the blue meter fills and they burn your cover. In a car you are fast and shielded, but they can always see you.

On a phone

Joystick on the left, buttons on the right, drag the screen to look. Hold the phone sideways.
