## Description
Attack helicopters are hunting you. Run, duck, dive-roll and hide through a 3D city, open fields and country roads. Buildings stop bullets, trees and tall grass hide you – but not for long. Drop into sewers, throw smoke bombs, or steal a car with a real manual gearbox. Every 25 seconds the choppers get angrier: more of them, better aim, rockets, then snipers. How long can you last?

## Instructions
Desktop:
WASD / arrows – run, Mouse – look, Shift – sprint, C – duck, Space – dive roll, J – jump,
E – manhole, Q – smoke bomb, F – get in / out of a car, G – taunt, P – pause, M – sound.
Driving: W/S gas and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake, Shift gear up, Ctrl gear down.
Mobile: joystick on the left, buttons on the right, drag the screen to look. Hold the phone sideways.
